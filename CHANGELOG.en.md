# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-30

- The extension now speaks Portuguese and English: the texts of the circle, menu, notices, copy window and extension window follow the browser's language.
- English documentation: `README.en.md` and `CHANGELOG.en.md`.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-30

- Shuffle the queue: shuffles the order of the "Up next" queue that is already playing, keeping the current song at the top.
- Save queue as playlist: creates a new playlist with the songs in the queue, in their current order, with the name and visibility of your choice.
- The circle also shows up outside playlist pages when a queue is playing.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-30

First version.

- Shuffle: plays the whole playlist in a shuffled order, with every song in "Up next", without changing the playlist.
- Save shuffled copy: creates a new shuffled playlist, with the name and visibility of your choice.
- Circle on the playlist page, with a menu, progress and notices.
- Extension window with an on/off switch and Automatic, Light or Dark theme.
- Unavailable and duplicate songs are left out.
- Support for large playlists, with the queue filled in batches while the music plays.
